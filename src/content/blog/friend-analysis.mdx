In 2023 I had the idea to rank all of my friends using data analysis. 
Here's a [template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1k2TPVh1GgXQjEeM1k4PU2jD0goi3XPM_ch7FKnRl96U/edit?usp=sharing) for you to do the same!

Some things you can find out from this exercise:
- Do you form closer friendships with a certain horoscope?
- Are there any trends in MBTI among your friends?
- Who is your best friend?
- Are you friendships close mostly due to high proximity?
- What is the age range of your friends?
- (if this analysis is done over different periods)
  - Who have you become closer to? Who have you drifted from?
- What makes you consider someone a close friend?
